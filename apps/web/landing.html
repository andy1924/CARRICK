A few practical details

Before you get started.

How Carrick fits into your team’s reporting and planning process.

Does Carrick update Primavera P6 directly?

The current workflow imports XER schedules and exports approved progress as CSV. Your planning team applies those records through its established process. Native schedule file output and direct P6 synchronization are planned capabilities.

How is AI used in the workflow?

When enabled, AI interprets field language and retrieves relevant activities from the imported schedule. A planner confirms the proposed activity and actual date. Standard matching is also available.

Which field reports can I upload?

You can enter a note, import a CSV field log, or upload a text file, email message (.eml), or PDF with selectable text. Scanned PDFs need OCR before import.

Is anything saved when I use the product tour?

The tour uses a synthetic pump station schedule. Your note is processed for the preview and is not stored. Reports submitted in the workspace are saved with their review history.
